Questions,answered.

Plain answers about BRUMA, peptides and the launch in Kuwait.

What is a peptide?

A peptide is a short chain of amino acids, the building blocks of proteins. Your skin makes its own peptides. In skincare, peptides are used to help skin look smoother, firmer and more even. BRUMA uses peptides in freeze-dried clinic serums (Fresh Activation) and in creams, serums and masks for your routine at home.

What does peptide skincare do?

Peptide skincare is used to help skin look smoother, firmer and more even, and to help it feel hydrated and comfortable. It is a cosmetic, not a medicine: it changes how skin looks and feels, and BRUMA makes no claims beyond that. The BRUMA range covers clinic serums and, for home, a Repair line, an Eye line and a Brightening line.

What is Fresh Activation, and why does BRUMA freeze-dry its serums?

Fresh Activation is how a BRUMA clinic kit works: the peptides are kept as a dry, freeze-dried powder and activated with a serum just before use, so the serum is fresh when it goes on your skin. Each kit has two vials: Part A, freeze-dried powder, 0.1 g, and Part B, serum, 5 ml. The clinic activates them before a treatment.

Are BRUMA products injected?

No. BRUMA products are for use on the skin only and are never injected. The clinic serums go on the skin after laser, skin boosters and other treatments, and the home creams, serums and masks are used like any other skincare. Every box says so, in English and Arabic.

Who are the BRUMA clinic kits for?

BRUMA clinic kits are for professional use in aesthetic clinics in Kuwait only, on the skin after laser, skin boosters and other treatments; they are not sold to consumers. There are four kits, N°01 Copper, N°02 Firm, N°03 Hydra and N°04 Tone, each with a freeze-dried powder vial and a serum vial. Clinics use them under the BRUMA label or their own, and BRUMA trains the clinic staff.

When and where will BRUMA be sold in Kuwait?

The BRUMA home products, the Repair, Eye and Brightening lines, will be sold online in Kuwait in 2027. Until then, BRUMA works with aesthetic clinics across Kuwait, which use the clinic kits during treatments and give the home products as aftercare. The clinic kits themselves are for clinics only. Is the full ingredient list available?

Yes. The full ingredient list is printed on every BRUMA box, in English and Arabic, so you can read it before you use the product. BRUMA products are cosmetics for use on the skin only. Registration of BRUMA products with the Kuwait Ministry of Health is in progress.

Is BRUMA suitable for sensitive skin?

Check the full ingredient list on the box and patch test first. Every BRUMA product lists every ingredient in English and Arabic, so you and your clinic can see exactly what is in it. If you have a skin condition or a known reaction to an ingredient, ask your clinic before using any skincare.
