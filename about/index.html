What BRUMA is

BRUMA is a clinic-grade peptide skincare brand from Kuwait, launching in 2027.

Who it is for

Aesthetic clinics, which use the Fresh Activation kits during a treatment, under the BRUMA label or their own.

And people in Kuwait who want the same care in their routine at home, by invitation first.

Standards
