Privacy.

What this site collects, why, and how to be removed.

What is collected

Your email address, if you type it into the sign-up pop-up or the invitation form.

Email addresses are kept on our own server, with the page and the date you signed up.

Your WhatsApp number and what you write, if you message BRUMA through a link on this site.

Anonymous visit statistics: which pages are opened and which buttons are pressed.

These set no cookies and hold no personal data. They are kept on our own server.

Why

To send you one message when the shop opens in Kuwait in 2027, if you asked for an invitation.

To answer what you asked on WhatsApp: a routine, a test kit, a creator application.

To see which pages and buttons are used, as counts only, never who used them.

Your details are not sold and not passed to anyone else for their own use.

How to be removed

Write to BRUMA on WhatsApp at +965 9764 4083 and your email address or number is deleted from the list.
